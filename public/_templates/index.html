03 / Lifetime

In the long term, they are all dead.

Every alias expires on its own. Every one has its max age.

One week by default.

Leave the lifetime blank and the alias lasts seven days. Shorter or longer is a number of seconds away.

?max-age=604800

Burn after reading.

Set the lifetime to zero and the alias is used up by its first read.

?max-age=0

Up to a month.

The max age is up to 2592000 seconds, including those burn-after-readings if never visited. 

?max-age=2592000
